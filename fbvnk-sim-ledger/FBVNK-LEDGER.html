Carnet de route — sim

Logbook

Home simulator record — not an EASA logbook. MSFS 2024, non-qualified device. Not flight time, not FSTD time.

The aeroplane files each leg on the Ramp tab and it writes its own row here — times, aerodromes, flight time, fuel and tacho, nothing typed. Columns, order, ruling and hand follow page 86 of NK’s own book.

Built from page 86. Claus sent the scan on 16 Sep 2026 (P_86.pdf) with: “the logbook needs to be updated to reflect my NK bookkeeping … I have made my own line with tacho time etc, not a total oil but as said depart and arrive, also I am using airborne (flight time) and not block time.” This page follows it: dates DD.MM with the year once at the head, his name written out on the first row and ditto marks under it, PIC in Fonctions, flight time HH+MM and airborne not block, the tank total in litres in Carburant Départ and Arrivée, the two Huile columns struck through, NIL in Incidents, and TACHO — his own column, ruled in by hand — as 1228,32.

The page is self-consistent on the two things this build leans on. Fuel: seven of its eight rows open at the litres the row above closed on, and the eighth steps 92 to 220, a 128 L uplift showing itself between two rows. Tacho: 1236,24 less 1228,32 is 7,9200 hours against 6,6667 hours flown, so the tacho runs 0,1567 h per leg more than the flight time — it turns on the ground, which is why nothing here derives one from the other.

The figure beside P1 in Nature du Vol is the running total of P1 time, kept exactly as the page carries it. Claus, 16 Sep 2026: “good bloody question… ha ha, I should know, but I just carried it over from the previous owners. Keep it the same”. So it is not this session’s reading any more — it is his instruction, and it stays.

Times

Written the way the book is written: dates day-month with the year once at the head of the column, aerodromes as bare ICAO, times four digits, flight time h:mm, the running total carried forward in Report and closed at the foot under a red rule. The Huile column is split Départ/Arrivée and holds what you write there in the real book: the FP-5L total on engine start and again at shutdown. Type both on the Ramp tab and they land here. Visa stays empty — nothing in the sim signs for customs. Totals are sim hours only; they never join or overwrite NK's real total time.
