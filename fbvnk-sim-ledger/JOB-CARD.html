Five parts also have no price in the ledger yet: carburettor, starter, oil pump and lines, exterior lamps, and oil per quart. The board already records the real lamps — Aero-Lites AL-3615X-PA landing, AL-3615X-F taxi, NavMax nav set, fitted June 2024 by IAC Coimbra.

The key that makes any route safe. NK_ST_ENG_HRS paired with NK_ST_MX_LOG is a cursor that only ever moves forward — the maintenance counter increments on every accepted page action and survives Reset to delivery. Store the last-seen pair against each posted invoice and posting becomes idempotent: no double billing across reloads, reconnects or a change of route.

Three ways to wire it

OptionWhat you seeFiles that changeWhat can break
A
in-sim page		 A LEDGER tab on the cockpit iPad beside HANGAR. Tap SERVICE on the cylinders, the invoice posts next to it. Money lands where the work happens. New html_ui/.../AH/LEDGER/ page, layout.json, and new persisted L:NK_LG_* in systems.cfg. One sim restart per build. systems.cfg [LocalVars] is the same file the wear model lives in. No CSV or year book on a small panel. The book exists only inside the sim.
B
standalone reader		 The ledger you have now, on a second screen, updating as you fly. No package change — but nk_telemetry.py gains about 30 datums, which breaks md5 7c14d946 and its review. A page opened from a file cannot fetch a local latest.json — browsers block it. Needs a localhost server or a paste step, on top of a logger whose first real load has not run.
C
hybrid		 A thin strip on the HANGAR page showing running total and cost per hour. The full book, invoices and export stay outside. Smallest package footprint: a few L:NK_LG_* and one strip. No new page. Two places to keep in step. The cursor above has to be right.

Recommended: C

It puts the fun where you look — a live cost in the cockpit — without pushing the whole book onto a panel that cannot export, and without making the ledger depend on a logger that is not proven yet. A is the better toy and the worse book. B is blocked on the file-fetch rule and a fresh review cycle before it starts.
